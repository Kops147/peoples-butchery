This guide provides an in-depth walkthrough of the The Peoples Autonomous Admin Portal. Every feature is designed to reduce manual labor and maximize profitability through AI-driven insights.

1. The Command Center (Overview Dashboard)

The moment you log in, you are presented with real-time business metrics.

  • Metric Tiles: Instantly view Total Customers, Total Revenue, and Pending Orders.
  • AI Today: This tile shows orders placed within the last 24 hours that the AI is currently managing.
  • Recent Order Stream: A live list of the most recent orders. Use the Status Quick-Select to manually override any AI decision.

2. Advanced Inventory & AI Stock Prediction ⚖️

Located in the "Inventory (kg)" tab, this is the most critical part of your operation.

  • Weight-Based Accuracy: Track individual cuts (T-Bone, Boerewors, Lamb) to 0.1kg.
  • The AI "Low Stock" Warning: Rows highlight in Red when stock hits the Min Level.
  • Self-Deducting Menu: Recipes automatically calculate and deduct raw meat weight from stock in real-time.
  • Add Stock: Enter the kg amount and hit +. The AI instantly recalculates your "Days Left" estimate.

3. AI Business Intelligence & Forecasting 📊

The "AI Reports" tab provides data traditionally unavailable to local butcheries.

  • Revenue Forecast: Predicts total revenue for the next 30 days based on growth velocity.
  • Stock Depletion AI: Provides an estimate of exactly how many days of stock remain for every item before you run out.
  • CLV (Customer Lifetime Value): Identify VIP patrons to reward with extra loyalty points.

4. Autonomous Dispatch & Order Management 📦

  • Tracking Stages: Pending → Braai → Packaging → Dispatched → Delivered.
  • Escalation Protocol: A ⚠ AI Escalated badge appears if an order exceeds SLA limits. Prioritize these immediately.

5. Financial & Credit System 💳

  • Lookup by REF: Find customers via unique ID (e.g., NL-2024-0001).
  • One-Click Credit: Simple entry for deposits with an audit log.

6. Troubleshooting & AI FAQ

Q: Can I stop the AI from automatically moving an order?
A: Yes. Manually changing a status hands over full control for that order.

Q: What happens if the internet goes down?
A: Current session data is safe due to local-first processing. Geocoding requires a connection.

Q: An order is marked as "Escalated" but the customer is happy to wait. What should I do?
A: Simply update the status, and the escalation badge will clear.

⚠ SAFETY NOTICE Admin sessions expire after 4 hours for security. Ensure terminals are locked during shift changes.
