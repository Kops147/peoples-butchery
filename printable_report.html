3. MASTER OPERATIONS GUIDE

The Peoples Autonomous Admin Portal Manual

The Command Center (Overview Dashboard)

The moment you log in, you are presented with real-time business metrics.

  • Metric Tiles: Instantly view Total Customers, Total Revenue, and Pending Orders.
  • AI Today: Shows orders placed within the last 24 hours that the AI is currently managing.
  • Recent Order Stream: A live list of the most recent orders. Use the Status Quick-Select to manually override any AI decision.

Advanced Inventory & AI Stock Prediction ⚖️

Located in the "Inventory (kg)" tab, this is the most critical part of your operation.

  • Weight-Based Accuracy: Track individual cuts to the tenth of a kilogram (0.1kg).
  • The AI "Low Stock" Warning: When stock hits the Min Level, the system highlights the row in Red.
  • Self-Deducting Menu: When a meal is sold, the AI automatically deducts the raw meat used from stock.
  • Add Stock: Enter the kg amount and hit +. The AI instantly recalculates your "Days Left" estimate.

AI Business Intelligence & Forecasting 📊

The "AI Reports" tab provides data traditionally unavailable to local butcheries.

  • Revenue Forecast: The AI predicts your total revenue for the next 30 days based on growth velocity.
  • Stock Depletion AI: Provides an estimate of exactly how many days of stock remain for every item.
  • CLV (Customer Lifetime Value): Identify your VIP patriots who spend the most over time.

Autonomous Dispatch & Order Management 📦

Traditional butcheries waste time calling drivers. Our AI handles the logistics.

  • Granular Tracking: Orders move from Pending → Braai → Packaging → Dispatched → Delivered.
  • Escalation Protocol: If an order stays Pending for too long, a ⚠ AI Escalated badge appears.

Financial & Credit System 💳

The Peoples is a Credit-First butchery to prevent payment friction.

  • Lookup by REF: Find customers via their unique ID (e.g., NL-2024-0001).
  • One-Click Credit: Enter deposit amounts and notes easily.
  • Audit Log: Every transaction is logged with timestamps and admin names for accountability.

Troubleshooting & AI FAQ

Q: Can I stop the AI from automatically moving an order?
A: Yes. Manually changing a status hands over full control for that order.

Q: What happens if the internet goes down?
A: Local-first processing keeps your current session data safe.

Q: How accurate is the "Days Left" prediction?
A: The AI requires 7 days of sales data to become highly accurate.

⚠ SAFETY NOTICE Admin sessions automatically expire for security. Lock your terminal during shift changes.
