DOLF & KOPS Custom 2-Stroke Delivery Fleet
Engineering Precision for Urban Pretoria — April 2026

At DOLF & KOPS Solutions, we don't just build software—we engineer the hardware that makes local delivery sustainable. Our custom-built, high-torque 2-stroke delivery bicycles are the backbone of the The Peoples delivery network.

1. Engine & Performance

  • Engine Type: Single-cylinder, 2-stroke, air-cooled.
  • Displacement: 80cc High-Torque variant (tuned for hill-climbing in Pretoria).
  • Max Speed: 45 km/h (Governed for safety and fuel efficiency).
  • Fuel Ratio: 20:1 during break-in / 40:1 standard operations.
  • Consumption: ~1.8L / 100km (Highly optimized).

2. Load & Cargo

  • Payload Capacity: Up to 35kg (Reinforced rear rack).
  • Thermal Insulation: Cargo boxes come standard with reflective heat lining to keep The Peoples cooked meals at serving temperature.
  • Reinforced Frame: Dual-gusseted steel frames designed for 100+ delivery cycles per week.

3. The "Autonomous" Connection

Every bike is equipped with a DOLF & KOPS Telemetry Unit that connects to the shop's AI Engine.

  • Route Optimization: The AI calculates the fastest route for the 2-stroke engine, avoiding unnecessary steep inclines.
  • Dispatch Pulse: The bike’s unit "pulses" when the AI marks an order as Dispatched, notifying the rider instantly.

4. Maintenance Protocol (Full Service Lease Only)

For partners on the Managed Fleet Lease, DOLF & KOPS handles all maintenance:

  1. Weekly Tuning: Carburetor cleaning and spark plug check.
  2. Bi-Weekly Chain Care: Tensioning and lubrication.
  3. Monthly Overhaul: Full engine inspection and carbon removal.
  4. On-Call Support: Mobile fix units available for breakdown support between 08:00 - 18:00 daily.
💡 WHY 2-STROKE? Simple maintenance, high torque-to-weight ratio, and immediate power delivery make it the most efficient way to get Boerewors & Pap to a doorstep while it's still hot.
