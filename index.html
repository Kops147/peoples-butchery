How It Works

Get fresh meat at your door in 4 simple steps.

1

Register

Create your account with your address and contact details. You'll receive a unique REF number.

2

Add Credit

Deposit to the shop and give the admin your REF number. They'll credit your account instantly.

3

Order Online

Browse raw meats and cooked meals. Add to cart and checkout — payment deducted from your balance.

4

Deliver or Collect

We deliver to you (charged at standard AA rates per km) or you collect from us at the shop.
